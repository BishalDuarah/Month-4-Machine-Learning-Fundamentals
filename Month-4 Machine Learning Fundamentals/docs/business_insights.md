## Key Business Insights
### 1.Tenure Impact
-> Non-churned customers: Avg tenure = 37.57 months
-> Churned customers: Avg tenure = 17.98 months
#### Insight:
Customers who churn have significantly lower tenure (≈ half).
Retention efforts should target customers within the first 12–18 months.

### 2.Contract Type vs Churn
| Contract Type  | Churn Rate |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| Month-to-month | **42.7%**  |
| One year       | 11.3%      |
| Two year       | 2.8%       |
Month-to-month customers churn massively more than long-term contract customers.
#### Business Action:
->Encourage long-term contracts.
->Offer discounts for 1-year upgrades.

### 3.Tech Support Impact
| Tech Support | Churn Rate |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| No           | **41.6%**  |
| Yes          | 15.2%      |
| No Internet  | 7.4%       |
Customers without Tech Support are nearly 3× more likely to churn.
#### Business Action:
->Bundle tech support with plans.
->Offer free trial support for high-risk users.

### 4.Monthly Charges Impact
->Non-churned: ₹61.27 avg
->Churned: ₹74.44 avg
Customers paying higher monthly charges are more likely to churn.
#### Likely causes:
->Perceived overpricing
->Value dissatisfaction
#### Business Action:
Offer loyalty discounts to high-paying customers.
