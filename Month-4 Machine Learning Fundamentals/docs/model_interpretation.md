## Business-Level Interpretation
### 1.Tenure Months
Customers with low tenure are significantly more likely to churn.
##### Business Meaning:
->First 12–18 months are critical.
->Onboarding & early engagement strategies matter most.
#### Retention Strategy:
->Loyalty incentives during first year.
->Early satisfaction surveys.

### 2.Contract_Month-to-month
Month-to-month customers are the highest churn risk group.
#### Business Meaning:
->Long-term contracts strongly reduce churn.
#### Retention Strategy:
->Offer discounts for 1-year or 2-year upgrades.
->Target month-to-month users with special plans.

### 3.Monthly Charges
Higher monthly charges increase churn probability.
#### Business Meaning:
->Price sensitivity is real.
->High-paying customers expect high value.
#### Retention Strategy:
->Loyalty discounts for premium customers.
->Bundle services instead of raising prices.

### 4.Total Charges
This is indirectly tied to tenure + monthly charges.
#### Business Meaning:
->Long-term high-value customers are less likely to churn.
->Low lifetime spend customers churn more.

### 5. Tech Support_No
Customers without tech support are high-risk.
#### Business Meaning:
->Service dissatisfaction drives churn.
->Support quality matters.
#### Retention Strategy:
->Offer free tech support trials.
->Promote bundled support plans.
